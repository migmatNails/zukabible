ZUKA · A-15 / A-16 · MESSAGE TEMPLATES + SCREEN SCAFFOLDS · DRAFT FOR FOUNDER REVIEW · 2026-07-18

How ZUKA speaks off-screen, and how screens are born.

A-15 fixes the words ZUKA sends when the app is closed — every template carries its consent basis (transactional · follow-push · marketing) and its channel budget. A-16 codifies the five scaffolds every existing screen already secretly follows, so future screens are assembled, not invented. Both are pure design law: no new backend surface, though delivery seams (FCM wiring, Africa's Talking SMS) stay named.

A-15 · Message templates — 10 messages, 3 channels, 3 consent bases

Laws (all channels): present-tense · real names + real times, never "an event" · UGX integer format · one idea per message · no emojis in SMS (GSM-7 budget), at most the state emoji in push · never marketing on a transactional or follow basis — a channel earns only what its consent covers (DPPA; §4.6 severance) · every money message names what happened, never what to feel · SMS may carry the holder's own ticket code to their own registered number ONLY (it's their credential; never to third parties — §12.3 boundary).

A-16 · Screen scaffolds — the five skeletons every screen composes from

Codified from what's BUILT (§1 Pulse, §6 Detail, §5 forms, §4/§8 lists, the sheets) — a new screen picks a scaffold and fills zones; it never invents placement. Universal law: every scaffold reserves the ribbon slot (offline/stale/error) at content top, states render in-place (never a blank page), primary actions live in the thumb zone.
