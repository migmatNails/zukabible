Why this exists: a borrowed phone, a dead battery, an elder without a smartphone — the SMS code (A-15 · S1) covers voice, this covers paper. Holder-initiated from §8's ticket ([Save PDF], secondary) — never auto-generated, never emailed unasked.

Reconciliation with §12.3 (recorded): the masking law governs receipts and shares — documents about a ticket. A-18 is the ticket. The holder exporting their own credential is the same act as showing their screen at the gate; the protections are token rotation (used = void) + the scanner's authority, not masking. The cash-line makes the risk transfer explicit.

No name by design: the scanner checks the token, not identity (M0); a printed name would add DPPA surface for zero admission value.
