A-21 · 02
The four tier classes — distinguishable at a glance
Squint test: Ordinary = quiet card · VIP = black-and-gold foil frame with die-cut corners · Table = forest ground with gold seat ticks · Vendor = hatched credential band. The luxury ladder is structure + material (sheen, foil, ghost type), all linear-gradient and typography tricks — cheap on a 2GB device, and nothing a cheaper tier can fake.
tier_class: ordinary The baseline. Clean, honest, unfussy — the other three are read against it.
tier_class: vip Noir foil: gold double hairline, die-cut corners, ghost VIP letterform, foil-gradient wordmark, gold price. Vibrant luxury without coral — gold is ZUKA energy, not cliché wrap.
tier_class: table · seats_per_unit = 8 Forest ground for the communal tier. "Admits 8 people" is first-class: eight gold seat ticks, a ghost ×8 watermark, a gold ADMITS 8 chip, and the price stated per table.
tier_class: vendor A working credential, not a party ticket: hatched safety strip + rotated VENDOR band over a utility card with a faint crosshatch. Distinct without borrowing VIP gold.

Rules: state which class owns the design, never why the Person's tier is lesser. No upsell, no nag, no "upgrade to unlock".
