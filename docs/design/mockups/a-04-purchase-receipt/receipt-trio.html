One receipt, three surfaces.

The receipt as one identity: the in-app record (A-04), the WhatsApp/IG share image (A-05), and the saveable document (A-06). Two laws bind all three: money facts are server-returned verbatim (amount_ugx, tx_ref — never client-computed), and admission credentials never leave the app — the QR never appears here, and ticket_ref is masked on every exported surface (it's a typed gate fallback, §10). The Flutterwave tx_ref is the receipt's public identity. Design-ahead: no receipt-read RPC exists yet (open question below).
