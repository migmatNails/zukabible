## What This Is

ZukaBibleV4 consolidates every architectural artifact ever produced for ZUKA into one
coherent, conflict-free, fully-developed system. It synthesises four source layers that
previously existed in parallel:

1. **ZUKA.md** — the 1,616-line original product vision (the founding document)
2. **Zuka-Bible 2 (root)** — Books I-XII, the constitutional layer
3. **zuka-biblev2 (subfolder)** — 65 Founder/Architect volumes (Standard, Expanded,
   BLACK, Deep editions) including the Crown Jewel OS specifications
4. **zuka-docs** — the canonical operational Volumes II-XXII

Every contradiction between these layers is resolved. All duplication is eliminated.
Every under-specified area is developed. The gaps none of them covered — the Attention
Economy, Security Engineering, the Experience Constitution, and the four missing
constitutional documents — are filled, with fresh research where the prior material
was thin.

## How To Read It

Read in three layers:

**Layer 1 — The Constitution (Volumes 01-03).** What ZUKA is, why it exists, the
doctrines, the red lines, the vocabulary. 30 minutes. Everything else is subordinate
to this.

**Layer 2 — The Architecture (Volumes 04-19).** The data model, the operating systems,
the experience and commerce layer, the intelligence layer. Read the volumes relevant
to your work.

**Layer 3 — The Platform & Governance (Volumes 20-24).** Infrastructure, security,
testing, legal, scaling. Read when building or deciding.

Start with `00_ZukaBibleV4_Master_Index.md` — it contains the full manifest, the
authority hierarchy, every contradiction resolution, and the MVP boundary.
