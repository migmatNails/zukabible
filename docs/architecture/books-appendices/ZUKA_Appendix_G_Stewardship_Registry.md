# ZUKA — APPENDIX G: STEWARDSHIP REGISTRY

> Source: Volumes III, V, VI, XIII | Zuka-Bible 2, Appendix G
> Stewardship Trust = Volume XIII, Part II Level 5

Stewardship is the act of taking responsibility for the preservation, continuity,
or flourishing of something larger than oneself. It is the highest form of
participation (Zuka-Bible 2, Book I Part B, Ch.6.2).

**Stewardship Trust (Volume XIII, Part II, Level 5):**
The highest and latest-available trust level. Only assessable after a person has
taken on responsibility for others — not merely accumulated their own participation capital.
Named here for the first time as a formal concept (Volume XIII, Part II was the first
explicit specification), retroactively naming a pattern already present across Volumes
III, V, and VI.

**Stewardship succession is always decoupled (Volume VI, Part XXII):**
No steward role transfers as part of a single event. Each role has its own succession
process, independent of the others.
