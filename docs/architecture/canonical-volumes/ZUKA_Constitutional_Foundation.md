# Part I — What ZUKA Is

---

## Chapter 1 — The Platform Identity

**ZUKA is a Human Participation Infrastructure.**

This sentence comes from Zuka-Bible 2, Book I Part A (Chapter 4.1) and Part E
(Chapter 24 — Supreme Constitutional Statement). It is the single most precise
statement of what ZUKA is, and it resolves the platform-identity question this
Bible carried as an open item across seven consecutive volumes:

```
"Human Gatherings" (Volume I) — correct at product level; too specific at
platform level. Gatherings are the mechanism; participation is the mission.

"Human Coordination" (proposed in Volume VII, deferred across Volumes
VII-XVII) — accurate but corporate-sounding. Coordination implies management.
ZUKA enables participation, which is richer and more human than coordination.

"Human Participation Infrastructure" (Zuka-Bible 2, Book I) — correct at
every level simultaneously. Infrastructure because it underlies everything.
Human because it serves people, not transactions. Participation because that
is the generative act — the thing that creates Memory, Relationship, Trust,
Community, Opportunity, and Legacy.
```

**Ruling on the platform-identity question (formerly carried as an open item
in the Founder Review Queue):**

"Human Participation Infrastructure" is adopted as ZUKA's internal
architectural identity — the answer to "what is this system?" that every
engineer, council member, and future hire should be able to give. It is not
a tagline or a product positioning statement; those remain the founder's
decision. But architecturally, this is what ZUKA is, and every volume in
this Bible is consistent with it.

# Part V — The Founder Red Lines

---

## Chapter 15 — What Cannot Be Compromised

From Zuka-Bible 2, Book I Part E (Chapter 22). These are the founder's personal
constitutional commitments, distinct from the operational ADRs — they are not
just architecture decisions, they are statements of who ZUKA is as a company.

```
REALITY BEFORE AI
  AI may interpret reality. AI may not replace reality.
  → ADR-003; Volume VII's entire DRL framework

TRUST BEFORE MONETISATION
  Revenue systems must strengthen trust.
  → A business model that weakens trust to generate short-term revenue
    violates this red line. Any pricing mechanism that lets money improve
    trust scores violates ADR-002 directly. Commission structures must
    not create perverse incentives that undermine organiser reliability.

HISTORY BEFORE CONVENIENCE
  Historical continuity is preserved whenever possible.
  → Volume VII's Temporal Truth Principle (append-only, never rewrite);
    Volume V's Memory-is-not-Media separation (ADR-009); Volume XIII's
    Appeals mechanism (append a superseding event, never edit the record)

COMMUNITIES BEFORE ALGORITHMS
  Algorithms support communities. Communities are not products.
  → Volume VI's community sovereignty principle; Volume X's Community
    Agent (cross-community patterns are surfaced TO communities, not
    used to optimise FOR engagement metrics at community expense);
    Volume XXII's prohibition on addiction-pattern design

PARTICIPATION BEFORE PROFILES
  Observed participation matters more than declared identity.
  → ADR-004; the entire DRL framework (you cannot claim capability
    you have not earned through actual evidence); Volume III's emphasis
    on Verification as earned (not purchased)

MERIT BEFORE VISIBILITY
  Discovery should reward contribution and trust.
  → Volume XXII's prohibition on fabricated social proof (dark pattern P1);
    the Opportunity Agent's matching logic (Volume X, Ch.7) explicitly
    uses trust evidence and participation history, not follower counts

SIMPLICITY BEFORE FEATURE COUNT
  Human understanding is prioritised over sophistication.
  → Volume XIV's Invisible Sophistication principle (the more sophisticated
    ZUKA becomes internally, the simpler it must become externally);
    Volume XXII's three-tap ticket purchase proof; the entire experience
    constitution
```
