---

# Chapter 1 — Experience Constitution

Experience is the human-facing expression of constitutional reality.

Users should experience value, not complexity.

---

# Chapter 2 — Experience Principles

- Life Before Features
- Progressive Exposure
- Invisible Sophistication
- Human Language
- Mobile First
- Accessibility First

---

# Chapter 3 — Human Experience Axis

Complexity
→ Abstraction
→ Story
→ Action
→ Outcome

The platform should reduce cognitive burden.

---

# Chapter 4 — Dashboard Constitution

Every dashboard shall answer:

1. What happened?
2. What is happening?
3. What should happen next?

---

# Chapter 5 — Personal Experience

Core Experiences:

- Identity
- Participation
- Memories
- Communities
- Opportunities

The platform should guide users toward meaningful participation.

---

# Chapter 6 — Discovery Experience

Discovery systems should prioritize:

- Relevance
- Trust
- Context
- Participation History
- Community Context

Discovery must remain explainable.

---

# Chapter 7 — Recommendation Constitution

Recommendations should answer:

Why am I seeing this?

Recommendations must be:
- Explainable
- Auditable
- Governable

---

# Chapter 8 — Storytelling Layer

Stories are preferred over metrics.

The platform should communicate:

- Progress
- Participation
- Community Impact
- Opportunities

through narrative experiences where appropriate.

---

# Chapter 9 — Accessibility Doctrine

The platform should support:

- Low Bandwidth
- Older Devices
- Multiple Languages
- Inclusive Experiences

Accessibility is constitutional.

---

# Chapter 10 — Mobile First Doctrine

Mobile experiences are primary.

Desktop experiences are extensions.

---

# Chapter 11 — Notification Constitution

Notifications should:

- Inform
- Guide
- Encourage

Notifications should never become spam.

---

# Chapter 12 — Experience Governance

Experience systems must remain:

- Explainable
- Auditable
- Trustworthy
- Governable

Experience may evolve.
Constitutional principles may not be violated.
