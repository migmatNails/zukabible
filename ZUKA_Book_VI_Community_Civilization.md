# Chapter 1 — Community Constitution

A Community is a persistent network of relationships organized around shared participation.

Communities are constitutional entities.

Communities are discovered and strengthened through participation.

- Persistent
- Participatory
- Trustworthy
- Governable
- Resilient
- Evolvable

# Chapter 5 — Community Trust

Community trust emerges from:
- Participation
- Stewardship
- Reliability
- Collective Contribution
- Historical Continuity

---

# Chapter 6 — Community Memory

Communities generate collective memory.

Community memory preserves:
- Stories
- Traditions
- Milestones
- Contributions
- Historical Identity

---

# Chapter 7 — Community Capital

Community Capital is collective value accumulated through sustained participation.

Community Capital influences:
- Trust Capital
- Opportunity Capital
- Legacy Capital

---

# Chapter 8 — Community Governance

Governance responsibilities:
- Participation Integrity
- Trust Preservation
- Stewardship Oversight
- Conflict Resolution
- Continuity Planning

---

# Chapter 9 — Community Stewardship

Stewardship is the active preservation and improvement of community health.

Stewards are custodians of continuity.

---

# Chapter 10 — Community Resilience

Communities should withstand:
- Member turnover
- Organizational changes
- Platform evolution
- External disruptions

---

# Chapter 11 — Community Federation Readiness

Communities may participate in federation only when:

- Governance maturity exists
- Trust maturity exists
- Continuity mechanisms exist

Federation readiness is capability-gated.

---

# Chapter 12 — Community Legacy

Communities may create enduring cultural, social, educational and institutional impact.

Community Legacy preserves collective participation across generations.
