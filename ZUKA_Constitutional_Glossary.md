# Constitutional Glossary

Identity: Persistent participant representation.
Relationship: Meaningful connection between entities.
Memory: Preserved meaning derived from participation.
Trust: Confidence earned through participation.
Community: Persistent network of participation.
Opportunity: Potential value creation pathway.
Legacy: Long-term preserved impact.
Stewardship: Active preservation and improvement of continuity.
Provenance: Traceable origin and transformation history.
Explainability: Ability to understand system outputs.
