# Chapter 1 — Temporal Constitution

Time is a constitutional dimension of reality.

Every entity, event, memory, trust state, relationship, community, and opportunity exists within time.

---

# Chapter 2 — Temporal Truth

Truth must be evaluated within its temporal context.

A statement may be:

- Historically True
- Currently True
- Future Projection
- Disputed

All truth claims require temporal classification.

---

# Chapter 3 — Event Sourcing Doctrine

Events are immutable.

State is derived from events.

Historical continuity shall be preserved whenever possible.

Core Principles:

- Append-only history
- Traceable lineage
- Auditable reconstruction
- Provenance preservation

---

# Chapter 4 — Knowledge Theory

Knowledge is structured understanding derived from reality.

Reality → Data → Information → Knowledge → Intelligence

Intelligence may never bypass reality.

---

# Chapter 5 — Canonical Knowledge Graph

Purpose:

Connect:

- Identities
- Relationships
- Memories
- Trust
- Communities
- Opportunities

The graph is a representation of reality.

The graph is not reality itself.

---

# Chapter 6 — Provenance Doctrine

Every significant record must maintain:

- Source
- Origin
- Timestamp
- Ownership
- Transformation History

Provenance is mandatory for trustworthiness.

---

# Chapter 7 — Data Governance Integration

Data quality dimensions:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Timeliness
- Integrity
- Provenance

No intelligence capability may exceed underlying data quality.

# Chapter 8 — Historical Continuity

Historical continuity is a constitutional principle.

The platform should preserve:

- Participation history
- Community history
- Relationship history
- Memory history

History may be protected, archived, or restricted.

History should not be casually destroyed.
