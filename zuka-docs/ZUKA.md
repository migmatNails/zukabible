## 1. Vision & Mission

### Vision
To become the definitive real-time entertainment intelligence and access platform for Africa — the single source of truth for what is alive in any African city at any given moment.

### Mission
To surface the full richness of Africa's entertainment economy — from the largest festival to the smallest bar going live on a Tuesday night — and make it discoverable, accessible, and enjoyable for every person in the city, while giving every organiser, venue, and artist the tools to build a sustainable live entertainment business.

### The Single Sentence
**Zuka tells you what's alive in your city right now, lets you tune in from anywhere, and gets you there safely.**

### Core Beliefs
- The best entertainment moment is the one you almost missed. Zuka makes sure you don't.
- Africa's entertainment economy is vast, vibrant, and almost entirely invisible to technology. That is the opportunity.
- Real-time is not a feature. It is the product.
- Discovery should have zero friction. Transactions should have minimum necessary friction.
- The city's data belongs to the city. We are stewards, not owners.

## 2. The Problem

### For Event-Goers
Every Friday night in Kampala, thousands of people spend 20–40 minutes asking the same question across WhatsApp groups: *"Where are we going tonight?"* The answer is fragmented across group chats, Instagram stories, word of mouth, physical flyers on lamp posts, and occasionally a Facebook post from 3 days ago. By the time a decision is made, the best moments are already happening without them.

There is no single place to ask: *"What is actually live right now, near me, that I would enjoy?"*

### For Organisers & Venues
A bar with a live band tonight, a rooftop that just filled up, a comedy night with 20 tickets left — none of these have a digital home that surfaces them in real time to the people most likely to show up. The options available are:

- **Facebook Events** — algorithm-gated, declining reach among youth, no real-time signals
- **Eventbrite** — built for Western markets, high fees, no East African infrastructure, holds funds until after the event
- **WhatsApp** — zero discoverability beyond existing contacts
- **Physical flyers** — effective but not measurable and not scalable

The informal entertainment economy — which represents the majority of entertainment activity in cities like Kampala — is entirely invisible to technology. No platform captures it. No data exists on it. No tools serve it.

### For the Broader Ecosystem
There is no reliable data on Africa's entertainment economy. Tourism boards, city planners, hospitality investors, brands targeting urban youth, and development organisations all operate on anecdote and intuition. The intelligence layer that could inform billions of dollars of decisions does not exist.

## 5. Core Product — Consumer Side

### The Zero-Friction Entry Principle
Browsing, scrolling, and viewing event details requires **no sign-up, no account, no friction of any kind.** Sign-up is triggered only when a user attempts their first action:
- RSVP
- Ticket purchase
- Save event
- Tune into audio stream
- Follow a venue
- Check in

Sign-up itself is one tap: phone number + OTP, or Google/Apple OAuth. No email required. No forms. No lengthy onboarding. The first action completes immediately after sign-up.

### The Home Feed — City Pulse
The primary screen is a **live pulse feed** operating on three simultaneous time layers:

**🔴 Live Now**
Events currently active with real-time signals: crowd level, audio stream available, Vibe Drops incoming. Sorted by proximity and personalisation score.

**🟡 Starting Soon**
Events beginning within 3 hours. Countdown timers. Ticket availability indicators. "Filling up fast" signals when capacity crosses 70%.

**🟢 Tonight / This Weekend**
Scheduled upcoming events browsable by category, neighbourhood, distance, vibe, and price range.

### The City Pulse Strip
The top of the home screen shows a live summary of city activity:
```
47 events live in Kampala    [bar chart of activity over last 6 hours]
```
This single element communicates the city's energy level before the user reads a single event name. If the number is high and the bars are rising, the city is alive.

### The Heat Map
One-tap toggle from list feed to a geographic heat map. Active venues glow amber/gold. Packed venues pulse coral. Dark zones are quiet. At a glance, the user knows exactly where in the city the energy is concentrated.

### Event Categories
Primary categories (one per event, required):
`Nightclub` `Bar` `Live Music` `Concert` `Comedy` `Sports Screening` `Movie Night` `Cultural` `Wedding` `Corporate` `Party` `Festival` `Religious` `Market` `Food & Drink` `Exhibition` `Theatre` `Wellness` `Conference` `Art Show`

Vibe tags (up to 3, optional — user-facing casual language):
`Lit 🔥` `Chill 😌` `Family-friendly 👨‍👩‍👧` `Date Night 💃` `Afrobeats` `Amapiano` `Jazz` `Gospel` `Hip-Hop` `EDM` `Live Band` `Free Entry` `Outdoor` `Rooftop` `Members Only` `18+` `All Ages`

### The Check-In System
User checks in at an event via the Smart Bucket QR or manual check-in button. Check-in:
- Confirms attendance (feeds crowd data into the heat map)
- Unlocks **Vibe Drop** — 15-second anonymous audio/video clip posted to the event's Live Wall
- Awards Pulse Points
- Enables real-time vibe rating: Quiet 💤 / Picking Up 🔥 / Popping 🔥🔥 / At Capacity 🚫
- Logs to the user's attendance history (private by default)

### The Live Wall
Each event has a Live Wall — a rolling mosaic of Vibe Drops from check-in users. Micro-moments from inside the event, aggregated in real time. Users at home can see the Live Wall without attending. This is the social proof engine that converts remote viewers into attendees.

### Personalised Pulse
After 3–5 check-ins, Zuka's recommendation engine begins learning:
- Which event categories the user actually attends (not just views)
- Which neighbourhoods they travel to
- Which price points they transact at
- Which vibe tags match their history
- Which day/time patterns they follow

The feed becomes a personalised reflection of real taste. Irrelevant categories recede. Relevant ones amplify. The app becomes increasingly irreplaceable as it learns.

## 6. Core Product — Host / Organiser Side

### Who Can List
**Anyone.** Zuka is an open platform. Any individual, business, venue, organiser, church, school, company, or community group can list an event. There are no gatekeeping requirements to publish a basic listing.

Two tiers of organiser status:
- **Open** — self-serve listing, basic features, no badge
- **Verified** — reviewed and approved, verified badge, unlocks premium features

### The Go Live Feature
The most important feature for the informal entertainment economy. Any registered venue or organiser can tap **Go Live** with a single tap. This instantly:
- Pushes a notification to all followers of that venue within 5km
- Bumps the venue to the top of the Live Now feed
- Activates audio streaming toggle (if sound system connected)
- Drops a live pin on the heat map with a pulsing animation
- Enables the crowd meter

No advance scheduling required. A bar that's unexpectedly full on a Monday night can go live and fill up further within 20 minutes. This is the informal economy feature that no existing platform has ever built.

### Crowd Meter
Venue updates crowd level manually (or inferred from check-ins):
`Quiet` → `Picking Up` → `Packed` → `At Capacity`

Users within 5km receive a push notification when a followed venue hits "Packed." This drives last-minute attendance decisions — exactly the moment that matters in nightlife.

### Event Types Supported
- **Standard event** — single date, single location, single ticket type
- **Multi-tier event** — multiple ticket categories (General / VIP / Table) with individual pricing
- **Recurring event** — weekly/monthly repeating events (e.g., every Friday)
- **Free event** — always free to list, no commission
- **Festival** — multi-day, multi-stage (Festival Mode — see §31)
- **Private event** — invite-only, link-based access
- **Hybrid event** — in-person + remote streaming simultaneously
- **Go Live session** — no advance scheduling, instant activation

## 7. The Audio & Video Streaming System

This is Zuka's most significant technical differentiator. No existing entertainment platform in Africa — or globally — provides direct audio access to what is playing at a live venue in real time.

### Mode 1 — Live Board-Out Audio (Venue-Streamed)
The venue connects Zuka's audio bridge to their sound system's output — either via:
- A hardware interface (future: Zuka Audio Dongle)
- A software bridge running on a device connected to the mixing board
- Direct integration with digital mixing boards that support streaming output

The audio is captured at the source — clean, high-quality board output — and streamed to Zuka users in near-real-time (target latency: under 3 seconds). Users hear exactly what's playing at the event, not ambient microphone noise from a phone in someone's pocket.

**Use cases:**
- DJ set live from Club Guvnor — users tune in from home and decide to go
- Live band at a rooftop bar — users preview the sound before making the trip
- Concert audio stream for users who can't attend but want the experience

**Conversion mechanism:** Users who listen for more than 2 minutes are shown a contextual prompt: *"Sounds good? 3 tickets left at UGX 15,000."* The audio stream is a purchase funnel.

### Mode 2 — Shazam-Style Track Identification
For events where the venue is not streaming their board, or for any event where the user wants to identify what's playing:

The user activates the Zuka Listen feature. The app uses audio fingerprinting (via integration with ACRCloud, AudD, or equivalent) to identify the track playing. The identified track is instantly queued on the user's linked Spotify or Apple Music account.

**Result:** The user stays musically in sync with the DJ's selection from anywhere — at the event, en route, or at home deciding whether to come.

### Mode 3 — Sync Screening (Movie Nights / Watch Parties)
For movie nights, sports screenings, or any video-content event:

The event host creates a Sync Session. Attendees and remote viewers join the session. The host controls playback — play, pause, skip, seek. All connected devices follow the host's control in real time (WebSocket sync, sub-200ms latency target). A chat layer runs alongside the video.

**Use cases:**
- Rooftop movie night: in-venue and remote attendees watch together
- Sports screening: bar hosts a Champions League watch party, users join the atmosphere audio stream while watching the match on their own screens
- Private screening: a film distributor tests audience response to a new release

### Mode 4 — Atmosphere Audio
A simpler version of Mode 1 for venues without professional sound systems. The venue places a Zuka-enabled device (a phone running the venue app) in a good acoustic position. The app streams ambient audio — crowd noise, music, atmosphere. Lower quality than board-out but zero hardware requirement. Accessible to every venue regardless of setup.

## 8. Ticketing System

### Ticket Classes
| Class | Description | Transfer | Splitting |
|---|---|---|---|
| `standard` | Single person, single entry | Yes (gift) | No |
| `group` | Multi-person, fixed group | Yes (partial) | Yes |
| `table` | Venue table reservation | Yes (by seat) | Yes |
| `vip` | Premium access, designated area | Yes (gift) | No |
| `streaming` | Remote viewing pass | Yes (gift) | No |
| `comp` | Complimentary issue | No | No |
| `staff` | Internal staff access | No | No |

### Ticket Tiers Per Event
An organiser can create up to 8 ticket tiers per event:
- Different price points (Early Bird, Regular, Late)
- Different access levels (General, VIP, Backstage)
- Different quantities per tier
- Time-limited availability per tier
- Capacity limits per tier independent of overall event capacity

### Ticket Provenance — The Closed System
Every ticket that exists in a Zuka bucket must have arrived through a Zuka-native process. The provenance system enforces this at the database level via a deferred constraint trigger — no ticket can exist without a valid origin record.

Valid origins:
```
purchase          — bought directly on Zuka
gift_transfer     — received as a gift from another user
promotion         — organiser promotional issue
points_redemption — redeemed via Pulse Points
comp_issue        — complimentary by organiser
group_split       — split from a group/table ticket
refund_reissue    — reissued after partial refund
migration         — legacy import (admin-authorised only)
```

Any attempt to inject a ticket into the system from an external source without an authorised origin record fails at the transaction level. The bucket cannot be poisoned.

### QR Token Architecture
Every ticket has a `qr_token` — a 32-byte random hex string. It is:
- Rotated on every significant state change (transfer accepted, check-in, return from escrow)
- Invalidated immediately when a ticket enters gift escrow (donor's QR dies the moment they gift)
- Re-issued fresh when a gift is rejected or expires (donor gets a new QR)
- Permanently nulled after check-in (used tickets have no valid QR)

## 9. Smart Bucket QR & Entry System

### The Concept
The Smart Bucket QR inverts the traditional ticket-scanning paradigm.

**Traditional:** User finds their ticket → presents ticket QR → scanner reads ticket → admits or denies.

**Smart Bucket:** User presents a single Bucket QR (always the same) → scanner reads bucket → server intelligently matches the bucket to a valid ticket for this event → admits or denies.

The user never searches their wallet. They raise their phone. The system does the rest.

### Bucket QR Format
```
ZUKA:BKT:v1:[40-char-random-token]:[HMAC-SHA256-integrity-hash]
```
- The token has no relationship to any user identifier
- The HMAC prevents QR forgery
- On its own the QR is meaningless without the server

### Scanner Session Binding
Every scanner session is bound at creation to a specific `event_id`, `gate_id`, and time window via a signed JWT. The scanner is **blind outside its session scope** — it cannot probe other events, other gates, or other time windows regardless of what QR it scans.

### The Matching Engine (Server-Side)
```
Receive: { bucket_token, scanner_JWT, timestamp }
1. Verify scanner JWT (signature, expiry, nonce)
2. Verify scan timestamp within session window
3. Verify bucket_token HMAC integrity
4. Lookup user from bucket_token
5. Query: tickets WHERE owner = user AND event = session.event
          AND status = 'owned' AND valid window active
6. If found: atomically check-in ticket, rotate QR, log
7. Return: admitted + minimum display data (name, ticket type, gate)
   OR denied (reason withheld from scanner)
```

The scanner **never sees bucket contents.** It receives only a boolean result and the minimum display data to process the person at the door.

### Display States
- ✅ **Green — ADMITTED:** Name, ticket type, entry zone, timestamp
- ❌ **Red — NOT VALID:** "No ticket for this event." No further detail.
- ⚠️ **Amber — ALREADY ADMITTED:** Gate and time of previous scan. Escalate to supervisor.

### Lock Screen Widget
After any ticket purchase, the app prompts the user to add Zuka to their device lock screen. The widget displays the Bucket QR. At the venue door, the user doesn't open the app — they raise their phone. Zero tap entry.

### Offline Capability
When the scanner loses connectivity, it operates against a locally cached manifest:
- **Zuka buckets:** Bloom filter (1,000 attendees ≈ 2KB, 0.1% false positive rate)
- **External tickets:** SHA-256 hash set (exact match, no false positives)
- **Capacity:** Last known count + local increment counter

All offline scans queue locally in an encrypted on-device database and sync to the server when connectivity returns. Conflicts are flagged for organiser review. Capacity overruns are tolerated over false denials — the trade-off is made deliberately.

## 10. Zuka Scanner Pro

### Overview
A full professional access control system embedded in the Zuka platform. Handles both Zuka-native tickets and external tickets from any source. Includes staff management, multi-gate coordination, real-time dashboards, and post-event reporting.

### External Ticket Support
The event must be listed on Zuka. The tickets do not need to be. Scanner Pro handles:

| Type | Validation Method |
|---|---|
| QR/barcode from other platforms | Scan → normalise → check against imported registry |
| Reference number ticket | Manual entry → check against registry |
| Name-based guest list | Fuzzy name search → operator confirms → admit |
| Physical paper ticket | Supervisor manual admit |
| Walk-in / comp | Supervisor override with reason tag |

### Staff Role Hierarchy
```
organiser
  └── supervisor (all gates, can override)
        └── gate_operator (assigned gates only, no override)
```

Each staff member receives a 6-digit gate PIN via SMS. No Zuka account required for gate operators — the PIN is their credential.

### Staff Management Controls (Real-Time)
From the organiser's dashboard:
- **Assign:** Add a staff member to the event with role and gate assignment
- **Reassign:** Move an operator between gates instantly
- **Promote:** Temporarily elevate a gate operator to supervisor
- **Suspend:** Immediately revokes scanning rights — takes effect on next heartbeat (≤30 seconds)
- **Remove:** Permanently removes from event

The **heartbeat system** runs every 30 seconds. Each scanner device pings the server. The server responds with the current role status. A suspended role means the next heartbeat locks the device and purges the offline manifest.

### Guest List Import
- CSV / Excel upload (flexible column mapping, auto-detected)
- Real-time manual addition during the event
- API push for organisers with their own systems
- Duplicate detection and rejection log
- Instant manifest refresh across all active scanners on import

### Organiser Live Dashboard
- Total admitted vs. capacity (live percentage bar)
- Admissions per gate
- Scan rate (admissions per minute — queue flow tool)
- Denial breakdown by reason
- Staff status (🟢 active / 🟡 idle / 🔴 offline / ⚫ not logged in)
- Live scan feed (last 20 scans across all gates)
- Conflict alerts (duplicate admits, capacity warnings, offline events)
- Remote gate controls (open, close, pause all)

### Post-Event Report
Auto-generated at event close:
- Admission summary by gate, ticket type, origin (Zuka vs. external), and hour
- Arrival curve chart
- Discrepancy report (duplicate attempts, offline conflicts, overrides)
- Staff performance metrics
- Revenue reconciliation (Zuka-native: sold vs. attended, no-show rate)
- Export as CSV

## 11. Gift Ticket System

### Core Principle
A ticket exists in one of two states: **owned** or **in escrow**. A gift places a ticket in escrow — it belongs to nobody until the recipient accepts or rejects. One ticket, one owner, always.

### Ticket State Machine
```
PURCHASED → OWNED
              ├── GIFT_INITIATED → [ticket locked, donor QR invalidated]
              │       ├── GIFT_PENDING_REGISTRATION (recipient not on Zuka)
              │       │       └── GIFT_PENDING_ACCEPTANCE (after signup)
              │       └── GIFT_PENDING_ACCEPTANCE (recipient on Zuka)
              │               ├── TRANSFERRED (accepted → permanent)
              │               └── GIFT_REJECTED (rejected → returns to donor)
              ├── GIFT_EXPIRED (72h elapsed → auto-returns to donor)
              └── CHECKED_IN (terminal)
```

### The Viral Acquisition Path
When a gift is sent to a non-Zuka user:
1. SMS + WhatsApp delivered to recipient's phone
2. Recipient taps link → mobile-optimised gift landing page
3. Page shows: event details, donor name, personal message, ticket visual
4. CTA: "Claim your ticket — download Zuka"
5. App download with `transfer_token` embedded as deep link parameter
6. On sign-up completion: gift surfaces immediately as first app experience

The recipient's first experience of Zuka is receiving something of value from someone they trust. Highest possible trust entry point. Zero acquisition cost beyond the SMS.

### Group & Table Ticket Gifting
A group ticket (capacity: 5) can be gifted as seat-level individual gifts. Each seat is a separate gift row with independent state. The ticket remains in the donor's bucket until all seats are transferred. The sum of seats in active gift rows can never exceed the ticket's stated capacity — enforced at transaction level.

### Security
- Transfer token is a 32-byte random string with no relationship to any ticket or user ID
- Tokens are single-use — invalidated on resolution
- QR rotates on every state change
- Rate limit: 10 gift initiations per user per hour
- Recipient phone validated against format rules and abuse blocklist before SMS dispatch

### Monetisation on Gifting
- **Gift wrapping:** UGX 2,000 for premium animated gift card with personal video message (up to 15 seconds)
- **Occasion detection:** If donor message contains birthday keywords, contextual upsell prompt appears
- **Viral LTV tracking:** Every non-user who signs up via gift link and purchases within 30 days is tracked as gift-attributed LTV

## 12. Zuka Ride

### Concept
SafeRoute — the end-of-night transport layer. Zuka surfaces verified transport options to users leaving events. Partners include SafeBoda, Farasi, Uber, and registered independent boda operators.

### Revenue Model — Charge the Driver, Not the Company
The commercial relationship is with individual drivers/riders, not their parent companies. This bypasses corporate negotiation cycles and creates a direct value exchange:

- Drivers pay a **monthly listing fee** (UGX 20,000/month at Uganda scale) to appear in Zuka Ride at event exit times
- Being listed means direct access to a high-demand customer stream at peak demand moments
- No per-trip commission — flat access fee only
- As Zuka's user base grows, the listing becomes more valuable → more drivers pay → more competition for placements → pricing power increases

**Compounding logic:** More users = more valuable listing = more drivers pay = better coverage = safer exits = more users trust the platform = more users go out more often.

### The User Experience
- After check-in, a "Getting home?" card appears in the user's event view
- At event close time, a push notification: "Need a ride home? Zuka Ride is ready."
- User selects their mode (boda, taxi, private hire)
- Nearby Zuka Ride partners shown with distance and price estimate
- Deep link to partner app (SafeBoda, Uber) for booking, or WhatsApp direct for independent operators
- "Share my route" — user sends live location to a trusted contact from within Zuka

### Safety Features
- Driver identity verification before listing approval
- User-reported driver ratings (kept internal — not public platform reviews)
- Incident reporting in-app
- Emergency contact auto-alert if user's location stops moving unexpectedly for 10+ minutes post-ride-start

| Redemption | Cost |
|---|---|
| Free entry to participating venue (off-peak) | 500 pts |
| Ticket discount (10% off) | 300 pts |
| Free drink at participating venue | 400 pts |
| Priority entry (skip queue) at participating venue | 250 pts |
| Early access to sold-out event waitlist | 600 pts |
| Streaming pass for one event | 200 pts |
| Gift a points boost to a friend | 150 pts |

### Economy Protection Rules
- Points never expire while account is active (no artificial urgency)
- Points cannot be purchased with cash (prevents inflation, maintains earned-only integrity)
- Anti-farming: check-in points require GPS proximity verification to the event venue
- Vibe Drop points require the drop to pass basic content moderation before awarding
- Maximum earn per day: 500 points (prevents single-day farming)
- Referral chain depth limit: 3 levels (prevents pyramid structures)

### The Venue Reward Pool
Venues purchase **Pulse Point packages** — blocks of points distributed to their attendees as loyalty incentives:

```
Package A — UGX 200,000: 2,000 points to distribute
Package B — UGX 500,000: 6,000 points (20% bonus)
Package C — UGX 1,000,000: 14,000 points (40% bonus)
```

Zuka takes a 20% margin on all package sales. The venue controls how points are distributed — per check-in, per ticket purchase, or as a one-time event bonus. This creates a three-way value loop: user earns → redeems at venue → venue gets traffic → venue pays Zuka for the points → Zuka distributes to users.

Before any data leaves Zuka for B2B consumption:

3. Differential privacy noise applied to small counts
4. Full audit log of every export (what, when, to whom)

### Data Governance
- Every database column tagged with: collection method, consent tier, sensitivity level, retention period

## 20. Search & Discovery Engine

### Search Surface
- Natural language search ("afrobeats tonight Kololo")
- Category filter
- Neighbourhood / distance filter
- Date & time filter (Now / Tonight / This Weekend / Choose date)
- Price filter (Free / Under UGX X / Any)
- Vibe tag filter (multi-select)
- "Near me" (uses GPS if granted, defaults to selected city zone)

### Ranking Algorithm (Feed & Search Results)
Events are ranked by a composite score:

```
score = (
  relevance_score       × 0.30   // category/vibe match to user profile
  + proximity_score     × 0.25   // distance from user
  + recency_score       × 0.20   // how soon / how live
  + social_score        × 0.15   // friend attendance, follows
  + quality_score       × 0.10   // venue verification, review rating
)
+ boost_modifier               // paid boost (capped — cannot exceed
                               // organic top 3 ranking by more than 2 positions)
```

The boost cap is the anti-drain principle applied to discovery: money can increase visibility but cannot buy the top result outright.

### Empty State Design
Zuka never shows a blank empty state. If no events match the current filter:
- Show the nearest matching results with the most restrictive filter relaxed
- Show what's happening in the nearest active neighbourhood
- Show "Explore [City]" — a curated selection of top venues currently active

*"Nothing live in Ntinda right now. Kabalagala is 2km away and has 8 active spots."*

## 30. Event Wallet

### Overview
An in-app prepaid balance system. Users top up with Mobile Money or card and spend on tickets with one tap — no payment confirmation required beyond the initial top-up.

### Why It Matters in the African Context
Card penetration is low. Mobile Money is ubiquitous but requires a USSD confirmation step that adds 20–30 seconds and a drop-off risk to every checkout. The Event Wallet collapses repeat purchases to a single tap by moving the friction to top-up time rather than purchase time.

### Technical Structure
- Wallet is a ledger (not a stored balance in a bank account sense)
- Every credit and debit is a ledger entry — immutable, auditable
- Wallet balance displayed in real-time in app header after first top-up
- Maximum balance: UGX 500,000 (Bank of Uganda payment regulation compliance)
- Minimum top-up: UGX 5,000

### Regulatory Positioning
The Zuka Event Wallet is classified as a closed-loop prepaid instrument (usable only on the Zuka platform for event-related purchases). This classification carries lighter regulatory requirements than a general e-money licence in most African markets.

## 34. Referral & Viral Growth System

### User Referral
Every user has a unique referral link and referral code. Shareable as:
- Link (opens Zuka app or web)
- QR code (scannable from any camera)
- Pre-formatted WhatsApp/SMS message

### Referral Rewards
- Referrer earns 200 Pulse Points when referred user completes sign-up
- Referrer earns additional 300 Pulse Points when referred user makes their first ticket purchase
- Referred user earns 100 Pulse Points on sign-up (welcome bonus, disclosed as referral reward)

### Chain Depth Limit
Referral chains are tracked to 3 levels maximum. Level 1 (direct referral) earns full rewards. Level 2 earns 50%. Level 3 earns 25%. Beyond level 3, no referral credit. This prevents pyramid structure formation while rewarding genuine community growth.

### Viral Sharing Touchpoints
- Every ticket purchase generates a shareable event card: "I'm going to [Event] — get your ticket before it sells out"
- Every Vibe Drop shared outside the app carries a link back to the live event
- City Pulse Score is formatted for one-tap WhatsApp share
- Gift ticket links are inherently viral (non-users encounter the platform through someone they trust)

### Farming Prevention
- Referral rewards only paid on accounts with verified phone numbers
- Self-referral detection: same device, same network, similar behavioural fingerprint → flagged
- Minimum 7 days between sign-up and referral reward being credited (gives time to detect fake accounts)

## 36. Legal & Compliance

### Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019
Zuka is designed to be compliant from day one:
- Explicit consent for all data collection (consent management system in schema from first migration)
- Data subject rights: access, correction, deletion — all available in-app
- Data breach notification: 72-hour notification to Personal Data Protection Office + affected users
- Cross-border transfer: data leaving Uganda requires adequate protection determination or contractual safeguards (B2B contracts include data transfer clauses)
- Data Protection Officer appointed before any marketing launch

### Financial Regulation (Bank of Uganda)
The Zuka Event Wallet is structured as a closed-loop prepaid instrument to avoid e-money licensing requirements. All payment processing is via licensed payment service providers (Flutterwave, Iotec) — Zuka does not hold or move money directly. Legal counsel review required before wallet launch.

### Age Verification
- Events marked 18+ or 21+ show age gate at ticket purchase
- Age verified against stored date of birth at account level
- Zuka is not liable for misrepresented age — liability disclaimer in Terms of Service
- Venues are responsible for physical age verification at entry

### Terms of Service Structure
- **User Terms:** Use of platform, content standards, ticket purchase terms, refund policy, intellectual property

- **Driver Terms (Zuka Ride):** Listing requirements, verification requirements, conduct standards, delisting conditions

## 37. Platform Architecture Principles

### Technology Stack (Recommended)
- **Mobile:** Flutter (single codebase, iOS + Android)
- **Web:** Next.js (server-side rendering for SEO on event pages)
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + Realtime + Auth + Storage)
- **Real-time:** Supabase Realtime subscriptions (live feed updates, crowd meter, Go Live notifications)
- **Audio streaming:** WebRTC (board-out) + HLS (fallback for low-bandwidth)
- **Video sync:** WebSocket-based playback synchronisation
- **Payments:** Flutterwave (cards + Mobile Money) + Iotec (Uganda Mobile Money direct)
- **Analytics/Data warehouse:** PostgreSQL views at MVP → ClickHouse at scale
- **Notifications:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (push) + Africa's Talking (SMS)
- **Maps:** Mapbox (heat map, event pins, customisable styling)
- **Audio fingerprinting:** ACRCloud or AudD API

### Data Architecture Principles
- Every database column tagged with consent tier, sensitivity level, retention period
- Event sourcing for all financial and ticketing records (append-only ledgers, no updates)
- Separate analytics database from operational database from MVP (prevents OLAP queries from affecting app performance)
- All personal identifiers hashed before appearing in analytics layer
- HMAC signing keys stored in environment secrets, never in database

### Performance Targets
- Home feed load: under 1.5 seconds on 4G
- Scan-to-admission response: under 400ms on stable connection
- Go Live notification delivery: under 10 seconds from tap to recipient push
- Offline scanner manifest: functional within 30 seconds of connectivity loss
- Payment confirmation: under 30 seconds for Mobile Money, under 10 seconds for card

### Security Principles
- All API endpoints authenticated (no unauthenticated writes)
- Rate limiting on all auth endpoints (OTP, sign-up, login)
- Scanner session JWTs signed with RS256, short-lived (session duration only)
- Transfer tokens are random, not derived from any identifier
- QR tokens rotate on every meaningful state change
- All PII encrypted at rest
- Database backups: hourly snapshots, 30-day retention, encrypted

## 40. MVP Scope

The MVP must prove exactly three things before anything else is built:

1. **A venue can go live and be discoverable within 60 seconds**
2. **A user can find something happening near them right now with zero friction**
3. **A ticket can be bought in under 3 taps**

### MVP Feature Set (Kampala Launch)

**Must have:**
- User sign-up (phone + OTP)
- Home feed (Live Now, Starting Soon, Tonight tabs)
- Event listing (basic — create, publish, free and paid)
- Go Live (instant activation)
- Ticket purchase (Mobile Money + card via Flutterwave)
- Smart Bucket QR
- Zuka Scanner (Zuka-native tickets only at MVP)
- Check-in (GPS proximity)
- Basic organiser dashboard (sales, check-in count)
- Push notifications (Go Live alerts, ticket confirmation)
- Venue verification (manual review process)

**Defer to Phase 2:**
- Audio streaming (Mode 1 and Mode 2)
- Gift ticketing
- External ticket import (Scanner Pro)
- Staff role management
- Pulse Points
- Zuka Ride
- Heat map (feed only at MVP)
- Group Planning
- Vibe Drops / Live Wall
- B2B data products
- Analytics dashboard (beyond basic counts)

### MVP Success Metrics (90 days post-launch)
- 50 verified venue listings in Kampala
- 2,000 registered users
- 500 tickets sold through the platform
- 20 Go Live activations per week
- Average scan-to-admission time under 5 seconds
- Zero ticketing fraud incidents
