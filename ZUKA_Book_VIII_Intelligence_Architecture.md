# Chapter 1 — Intelligence Constitution

Intelligence is the structured amplification of human participation through knowledge, reasoning and contextual assistance.

Reality precedes intelligence.

---

# Chapter 2 — Intelligence Principles

Intelligence shall be:

- Explainable
- Governable
- Auditable
- Safe
- Human-Centered
- Capability-Gated

---

# Chapter 3 — AI Governance

AI systems must:

- Respect constitutional principles
- Preserve trust
- Remain explainable
- Remain auditable

AI may assist.
AI may not govern constitutional reality.

---

# Chapter 4 — AI Twins

AI Twins are persistent contextual intelligence representations.

AI Twins remain dormant until:

- DRL readiness exists
- Governance approval exists
- Explainability requirements are satisfied

---

# Chapter 5 — Distributed Agents

Agents perform bounded tasks on behalf of participants.

Agents require:

- Scope limitations
- Audit trails
- Governance controls
- Capability readiness

---

# Chapter 6 — Historians

Historians interpret preserved knowledge and memory.

Historians support:

- Continuity
- Discovery
- Contextual understanding

Historians do not rewrite history.

---

# Chapter 7 — Copilots

Copilots assist human decision-making.

Copilots:

- Recommend
- Explain
- Guide

Copilots do not replace human agency.

---

# Chapter 8 — Knowledge Intelligence

Knowledge Intelligence derives insights from:

- Data
- Events
- Relationships
- Communities
- Memories

Knowledge Intelligence must remain grounded in reality.

---

# Chapter 9 — Trust Intelligence

Trust Intelligence remains dormant until:

- DRL maturity exists
- Trust safeguards exist
- Explainability requirements are satisfied

Trust Intelligence may never override constitutional trust principles.

---

# Chapter 10 — Opportunity Intelligence

Opportunity Intelligence helps identify potential pathways for value creation.

Opportunity Intelligence must:

- Prioritize fairness
- Preserve trust
- Remain explainable

---

# Chapter 11 — Explainability Constitution

Every significant intelligence output should answer:

- Why?
- Based on what?
- With what confidence?
- Subject to what limitations?

---

# Chapter 12 — Intelligence Safety

Safety controls include:

- Governance review
- Capability gating
- Auditability
- Human oversight
- Risk assessment

---

# Chapter 13 — Dormant Capability Governance

Architected does not mean activated.

Capabilities may remain dormant for years until readiness conditions are met.

---

# Chapter 14 — Intelligence Evolution

Intelligence evolves through:

- DRL progression
- Governance approval
- Constitutional compliance
- Explainability validation
