
# ZUKA Architecture Bible

# Volume XXVII (Expanded)
# Experience Architecture & Dashboard Constitution

## Executive Summary

The success of ZUKA depends on transforming deep internal sophistication into extreme external simplicity.

Internally:
- Trust OS
- Community OS
- Opportunity OS
- DRL
- CRL
- Security Systems
- Intelligence Systems

Externally:
A simple, intuitive experience.

---

# Experience Constitution

## Life Before Features
Users care about experiences, events, communities, memories and opportunities.

## Progressive Exposure
Show only what matters now.

## Growth Not Exclusion
Capabilities should appear to grow, not unlock abruptly.

## Invisible Sophistication
Internal Complexity ↑
External Complexity ↓

## Human Language
Never expose raw scores or internal models.
