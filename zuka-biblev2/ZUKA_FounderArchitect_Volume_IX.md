
# ZUKA Architecture Bible — Founder/Architect Edition
# Volume IX — AI Twin Architecture

## Mission
Create contextual intelligence, not generic AI.

## Twin Types
- Personal Twin
- Family Twin
- Community Twin
- Gathering Twin
- Opportunity Twin
- City Twin

## Twin Constitution
A twin may never claim confidence greater than the supporting data.

## DRL Activation Matrix

### DRL 0-2
Historical representation only.

### DRL 3
Insights.

### DRL 4
Recommendations.

### DRL 5
Historians, copilots and predictive systems.

## Governance
Users can:
- Enable
- Disable
- Export
- Delete

## Safety
Twins cannot make:
- Legal decisions
- Financial decisions
- Governance decisions
without human oversight.

## Founder Notes
Twins consume the graph.
Twins never become the source of truth.
