# Mission

Identity is the foundation of ZUKA.

Everything else is built on top of identity:

Identity
→ Participation
→ Memories
→ Trust
→ Communities
→ Opportunities
→ Governance

If Identity is weak:

Everything above it becomes weak.

---

# Identity Constitution

## Principle of Identity Continuity

Identity is not a profile.

Identity is a continuously evolving representation of a person.

---

## Principle of Identity Ownership

Users own their identity.

The platform stewards it.

---

## Principle of Identity Portability

Identity should not be trapped.

Users must retain control over their data.

---

## Principle of Identity Growth

Identity evolves through participation.

Not through form filling.
