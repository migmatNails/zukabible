
# Volume XXXII — MVP Architecture Blueprint & Service Boundaries

## Mission
Translate the ZUKA Constitution into a buildable MVP.

## MVP Scope
- Event Discovery
- Venues
- Ticketing
- Identity
- Memories
- Communities (seeded)
- Trust Signals (seeded)

## Service Boundaries
- Identity Service
- Event Service
- Ticketing Service
- Venue Service
- Community Service
- Memory Service
- Notification Service
- Payment Service
- Analytics Service

## DRL/CRL Gating
Features may exist but remain dormant until readiness conditions are met.

## Founder Principle
Build for today's users while preserving tomorrow's architecture.
