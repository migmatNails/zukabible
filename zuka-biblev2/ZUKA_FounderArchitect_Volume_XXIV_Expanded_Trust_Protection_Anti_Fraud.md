
# Volume XXIV (Expanded) — Trust Protection & Anti-Fraud Architecture

## Mission
Protect trust as a platform asset.

## Threat Model
- Fake Accounts
- Sybil Networks
- Ticket Fraud
- Review Manipulation
- Trust Farming
- Referral Abuse
- Community Capture
- Payment Fraud

## Trust Integrity Engine
Signals:
- Identity
- Participation
- Longevity
- Consistency
- Community Endorsement

## Fraud Lifecycle
Detection
→ Investigation
→ Containment
→ Appeal
→ Resolution

## Anti-Sybil Strategy
- Device Fingerprints
- Behavioral Analysis
- Network Analysis
- Relationship Validation

## Explainable Enforcement
Every action must answer:
- Why?
- Evidence?
- Confidence?
- Appeal Path?

## Trust Protection DRL/CRL
Trust protections mature progressively with ecosystem maturity.

## Founder Rule
Protect legitimate users without creating friction for honest participants.
