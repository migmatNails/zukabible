
# ZUKA Founder/Architect BLACK EDITION
# Volume XXIV — Trust Protection & Anti-Fraud (Implementation Blueprint)

## Mission

Protect the Trust Graph.

Everything in ZUKA eventually depends on trust:

Identity
→ Participation
→ Reputation
→ Communities
→ Opportunities
→ Economic Value

If trust is compromised, the platform is compromised.

---

# Anti-Fraud Constitution

## Principle of Trust Preservation

Protect honest users.

Detect dishonest actors.

Avoid unnecessary friction.

---

## Principle of Explainable Enforcement

Every action must answer:

- Why?
- Based on what evidence?
- At what confidence?
- What appeal path exists?

---

## Principle of Progressive Enforcement

Observe
→ Flag
→ Restrict
→ Suspend
→ Ban

Never jump to maximum punishment without justification.
