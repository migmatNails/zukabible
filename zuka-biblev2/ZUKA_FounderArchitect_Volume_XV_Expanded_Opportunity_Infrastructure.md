# Opportunity Constitution

## Principle of Earned Opportunity

Opportunities should emerge from:

- Participation
- Trust
- Contribution
- Reputation
- Community Value

Not merely visibility.

## Principle of Explainable Opportunity

Every recommendation must answer:

- Why this person?
- Why now?
- Which signals contributed?
- What confidence exists?

## Principle of Opportunity Fairness

Opportunity exposure must be governed by:

- DRL
- CRL
- Trust Signals

Not by popularity alone.
