# ZUKA — Plain Language Overview

## What is ZUKA?

ZUKA is a community, beauty, memories, trust and opportunity platform. It starts by helping people discover beauty professionals, book services and participate in communities. Over time it evolves into a Human Coordination Platform.

## What Problems Does It Solve?

- Finding trusted service providers
- Managing communities
- Preserving memories
- Building trust
- Discovering opportunities
- Organizing gatherings and events

## Initial MVP

Focus:
- Nail technicians

Capabilities:
