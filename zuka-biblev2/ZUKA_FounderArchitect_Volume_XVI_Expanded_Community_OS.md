# Volume XVI (Expanded) — Community OS Architecture

## Mission

Every community is a living operating system.

ZUKA does not manage groups.

ZUKA manages:

- Families
- Churches
- Schools
- Alumni Networks
- Clubs
- Associations
- Creator Communities
- Villages
- Cities

through a unified Community OS.

# Community OS Thesis

Communities are the fundamental coordination units of civilization.

Gatherings create communities.

Communities create trust.

Trust creates opportunities.

Opportunities create economies.
