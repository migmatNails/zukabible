# Volume VII — Temporal Architecture

## Mission
Time is a first-class architectural domain. ZUKA never overwrites meaningful history.

## Architectural Principles
1. Principle of Temporal Truth
2. Principle of Historical Continuity
3. Principle of Data-Led Evolution
4. Principle of Immutable Events

## Four Clocks

- Observation Clock
- Processing Clock
- Knowledge Clock

## Event Sourcing Standard
All meaningful state transitions produce immutable events.

Examples:
- IdentityCreated
- CommunityJoined
- GatheringAttended
- MemoryCreated
- TrustGranted
- OpportunityAccepted

### DRL 2

### DRL 3

### DRL 4

### DRL 5

## Feature Seed Architecture
Every future feature must define:
- Required entities
- Required events
- Required signals
- DRL threshold
- Activation criteria
