# ZUKA Architecture Bible — Founder/Architect Edition

# Volume XIII (Expanded) — Trust Infrastructure & Governance Architecture

## Mission

Trust is the invisible infrastructure of human coordination.

Identity creates participation.
Participation creates trust.
Trust creates opportunities.
Opportunities create economies.

Without trust, the ecosystem collapses.

---

# Trust Constitution

## Principle of Earned Trust

Trust is earned through behavior.

Never through claims.

---

## Principle of Explainable Trust

Every trust outcome must answer:

- Why?
- Based on which signals?
- Over what period?
- At what confidence?

---

## Principle of Temporal Trust

Trust is a journey.

Trust history matters more than trust state.

---

## Principle of Contextual Trust

Trust is domain specific.

Examples:

- Event Trust
- Financial Trust
- Leadership Trust
- Community Trust

---

# Trust Decay

Trust is not permanent.

Long inactivity may reduce confidence.

Decay must be:

- Explainable
- Gradual
- Reversible

# Trust Inheritance

Trusted Community
→ New Subcommunity

Trusted Organization
→ New Initiative

Inheritance is never absolute.
