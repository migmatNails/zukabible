# Chapter 22 — Founder Red Lines

AI may interpret reality.
AI may not replace reality.

## Trust Before Monetization

## History Before Convenience
Historical continuity is preserved whenever possible.

## Communities Before Algorithms
Algorithms support communities.
Communities are not products.

## Participation Before Profiles
Observed participation matters more than declared identity.

## Merit Before Visibility
Discovery should reward contribution and trust.

## Simplicity Before Feature Count
Human understanding is prioritized over sophistication.
